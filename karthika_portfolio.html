About me

Data analyst with a passion for insight

I'm a results-driven Senior Data Analyst with 3+ years of experience transforming complex financial and business datasets into strategic insights. I've worked across insurance, media, e-commerce and banking — always focused on delivering trusted, well-governed data that enables better decisions.
